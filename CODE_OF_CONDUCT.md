# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Effectprint contributors, maintainers, and participants commit to making project spaces respectful, welcoming, and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical claims with evidence.
- Respect differing viewpoints and the safety constraints of the project.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus criticism on work, not people.
- Protect private information and use only harmless, fake data in reproductions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Encouraging unsafe demonstrations against systems without authorization.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue. Use GitHub's private reporting controls when available; if maintainer review is required, open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/TommyTranX/effectprint/security/advisories/new) and clearly label it as a Code of Conduct report.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect the reporter's privacy where possible, and take proportionate action. Responses may include a private correction, warning, temporary restriction, removal from project spaces, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who are the subject of a report must not participate in resolving it.
